## Brand & Style

The design system embodies high-precision geospatial engineering, aerial drone telemetry, and LiDAR photogrammetry. It balances mission-critical instrument ergonomics with high-tech computational aesthetics. It targets spatial researchers, defense and mining compliance authorities, agricultural intelligence directors, and GIS architects.

The visual style is **Cyber-Spatial Telemetry**: an exacting synthesis of mission HUD (Heads-Up Display) overlays, dark navy oceanic depths, and luminous categorical vector accents. Crisp grid-aligned cards mimic tactical avionics consoles, using frosted multi-layered glass, luminous boundary wireframes, and dense alphanumeric metadata tags. The interface communicates surgical spatial accuracy, absolute real-time reliability, and frontier photogrammetric depth.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an abyss-depth tactical dark mode. Pure `#000000` and deep oceanic slate `#050b14` provide the absolute ground layer, preventing optical fatigue during intense raster and 3D point cloud inspections. 

Primary brand and action surfaces deploy electric telemetry cyan (`#38bdf8` and `#0ea5e9`), representing active laser scans and flight paths. Color fulfills strict domain taxonomy:
- **Telemetry Cyan (`#38bdf8`):** Active sensor systems, interactive viewports, and primary trigger controls.
- **Spectral Green (`#10b981`):** Vegetation index (NDVI), canopy density, and verified ground-truth validations.
- **Heritage Amber (`#f59e0b`):** Archaeological structures, high-value cadastral markers, and Rule 34A mine alerts.
- **Elevation Crimson (`#f43f5e`):** Digital Elevation Model (DEM) extrema, steep slope thresholds, and emergency warnings.
- **Mesh Violet (`#a855f7` / `#8b5cf6`):** 3D reality capture, textured wireframes, and spatial interpolation engines.

All chromatic elements must maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast against `#050b14` and `#0b1528`. Never place saturated accent text over tinted translucent fills without full contrast validation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear structural tension between tactical, condensed headlines and clinical body prose:

1. **Oswald (Display & Headlines):** Rendered in uppercase or assertive title case with deliberate tracking. Oswald provides the compact, urgent cadence of aerospace monitors and flight deck instruments.
2. **Inter (Body & Narrative):** Neutral, exceptionally clear at small scales, handling lengthy spatial project summaries, technical methodologies, and analytical documentation.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Metadata, Eyebrows & Coordinates):** Used for category badges, geographic bounding coordinates, sensor accuracy markers (e.g., `< 5cm GSD`), and HUD status alerts. Always set with generous tracking (`0.1em` to `0.14em`) to maintain legibility in low-light environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a strict 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width: `1440px`), compressing to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column flow on mobile (`< 768px`).

Grid layouts use a baseline unit of 8px (0.5rem) with 4px sub-increments for fine HUD elements. Spacing maintains technical order:
- **Section Margins:** `space-xl` to double `space-xl` creates intentional negative space between telemetry decks, letting dense visualization models breathe.
- **Card Internals:** Strict 24px (`space-lg`) internal gutters separate card headers, preview viewports, and launch footers.
- **Micro Gaps:** 4px (`space-xs`) and 8px (`space-sm`) are reserved for metadata status chips, pill icons, and coordinate readouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical-computational stratification rather than conventional soft drop shadows:

1. **Layer 0 (Base Substrate):** Deep `#050b14` with a subtle SVG isometric coordinate grid (`stroke: rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.05)`, 32px cell size).
2. **Layer 1 (Telemetry Panels & Cards):** Glassmorphic panels backed by `rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an internal 1px border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.16)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Interactive Hover & Focus):** Targeted borders illuminate to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.45)` paired with a directional glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)`.
4. **Layer 3 (HUD Floats & Floating Modals):** Pinned persistent heads-up widgets (e.g., the bottom-right Spatial Assistant) hover over all views with `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 16px 1px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced `rounded-2` geometry (8px / 0.5rem base radius) to evoke rugged tactical tablets and modern instrument panels.

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 12px to 16px (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`) outer corner radius with inset clip-path viewports.
- **Chips, Badges, and HUD Buttons:** 4px to 6px (`rounded-sm` / `rounded-md`) to preserve an architectural, precision-machined aesthetic.
- **Floating Sensor Triggers & Orb Indicators:** Full circular forms (`9999px`) to immediately signal floating, omnidirectional radar or connectivity status.

## Components

### Buttons & App Triggers
- **Primary Spatial CTA:** Full chromatic fill (matching domain tint: cyan, amber, green, or red) with high-contrast text (`#050b14` or `#ffffff`), accompanied by an inline directional glyph (e.g., right-caret or external link). Height: 44px; padding: 0 18px; font-weight: 600.
- **Secondary / Ghost Console Actions:** Transparent background, 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), hovering to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)` fill with illuminated text.
- **App Launcher Group:** High-contrast primary action paired with a square external icon button (`44px x 44px`) with a shared 1px border boundary.

### Telemetry Cards
- Structured in three vertically segmented zones:
  1. *Header Zone:* Category eyebrow (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px uppercase) housed in a dark pill badge, positioned above a subtle horizontal separator.
  2. *Graphic Viewport:* Aspect ratio 16:9 or 2:1 featuring subtle background coordinate grids, centered vector domain glyphs, and thematic gradients (e.g., cyan-to-dark for raster, violet-to-dark for 3D mesh).
  3. *Action Footer:* H3 headline, 3-line descriptive copy, and dual-button launch triggers pinned to the card base.

### Domain Chips & Metadata Badges
- Set with `JetBrains Mono` at 11px. Dark translucent background (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`), 1px solid border matching the domain category color, and 4px 10px padding.

### Input Fields
- Dark container fill (`rgba(5, 11, 20, 0.8)`), 1px border (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`), focus state shifting to pure cyan glow (`#38bdf8`) with zero outline displacement. Label in all-caps mono (`11px`) with required flags signaled in heritage amber (`#f59e0b`).

### Floating HUD Orb & Spatial Assistant
- Fixed floating orb at bottom right (`56px x 56px`), surrounded by a pulsing radar wave ring. Emits real-time operational status with a 6px green LED status beacon (`#10b981`).
